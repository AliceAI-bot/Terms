# Privacy Policy for Alice

**Effective Date:** 17/09/2026
**Last Updated:** 17/09/2026

> This Policy explains what data Alice handles, why, who else processes it, how long it is kept, and your choices.

## 1) Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("**Policy**") describes how Alice ("**Alice**," "**we**," "**our**," "**us**") processes data when providing its Discord bot services. By using the Services, you acknowledge this Policy. Use of the Services is also governed by our Terms of Service.

## 2) Scope

This Policy applies to data processed through Alice's chat, persona, memory, tool, premium, moderation, and support features. It does not cover Discord itself, Google AI Studio, or Top.gg, which operate under their own policies.

## 3) Definitions

- **"Services"**: the Alice Discord bot and related operational systems.
- **"User Content"**: prompts, messages, commands, attached images, links, and related metadata you submit.
- **"Processing"**: any operation on data (collection, use, storage, disclosure, deletion, etc.).
- **"Inference Provider"**: third-party AI infrastructure, namely **Gemma via Google AI Studio**.

## 4) Data We Process

Only what is reasonably necessary to operate the service:

### 4.1 Platform Identifiers

- User IDs, guild IDs, channel IDs.

### 4.2 Chat and Content Data

- Message text and attached images (up to 2 per message) sent to Gemma to generate replies.
- Recent conversation history (up to 30 messages per channel) and rolling summaries used for context.

### 4.3 Profile and Preference Data

- ToS acceptance timestamp, account tier (`free` / `premium` / `byok`), premium expiry.
- Message count, first-seen date, badges.
- Relationship state (affection score, status, last emotion, interaction timestamps).
- Memories (up to 30) — stored **only** when you ask Alice to remember something, and removable on request.
- Your own API key, if you provide one (BYOK) — stored **encrypted**, removable at any time via the BYOK remove option.

### 4.4 Moderation and Safety Data

- Terms acknowledgment status, blacklist flag and reason (reason stored **encrypted**).
- Ignore states, usage counts, vote-status cache.

### 4.5 Support Data

- Information you provide through approved support channels.

We do not intentionally collect unnecessary profile data, and no payments are processed through Alice, so no payment data is collected.

## 5) Purposes

Data is processed only for limited purposes: generating replies and keeping conversation context; remembering what you asked to be remembered; tracking relationship continuity; enforcing quotas and voter bonuses; safety filtering, abuse prevention, and blacklist enforcement; debugging and reliability; responding to support requests; and legal compliance.

## 6) Legal Bases (Where Required)

Depending on jurisdiction: performance of the service relationship, legitimate interests (safety, reliability, fraud prevention), compliance with legal obligations, and consent where required.

## 7) Third-Party Processing

- **Gemma via Google AI Studio** — receives User Content and necessary context to generate AI responses, including attached images.
- **Top.gg** — receives your user ID to check vote status for bonus usage.
- **Discord** — the underlying platform; all interactions pass through it.
- If you use BYOK, your key is sent to Google AI Studio alongside your requests.

We do **not** sell personal data and run no advertising.

## 8) Disclosure

Limited data may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to: operate the Services via the providers above; enforce safety rules and the Terms; detect or investigate fraud and abuse; comply with lawful requests or legal obligations; or protect the rights, safety, and property of users or us.

## 9) Flagging and Moderation Signals

Some interactions may be automatically flagged by safety controls. Associated metadata may trigger refusals or ignores, detect repeat abuse, apply restrictions, or support compliance actions. A flag is an operational indicator, not a legal determination.

## 10) Retention

- Channel chat history and summaries: expire after about **3 hours** of inactivity.
- Daily usage counters and vote caches: short-lived (hours).
- Ignore states: about **10 minutes**.
- Profiles (tier, stats, relationship, memories, BYOK key): persist until you request deletion, so Alice remembers you between sessions.
- Safety and blacklist records: may be retained as long as necessary for security, enforcement, and legal compliance, even after a deletion request.

## 11) Deletion Requests

Subject to legal, security, and abuse-prevention needs, you may request deletion of your data via the contact channels below. Ask Alice to forget specific memories any time in chat; remove your BYOK key via the BYOK remove option. Where full deletion is not possible (e.g. active safety records), we retain only the minimum necessary data.

## 12) Security

User IDs are hashed in storage and sensitive fields (BYOK keys, blacklist reasons) are encrypted. We apply reasonable safeguards against unauthorized access or loss, but no system is fully secure. You are responsible for your own Discord account security and for keeping any API keys you provide safe.

## 13) International Processing

Infrastructure and providers may operate in multiple jurisdictions, so data may be processed outside your local jurisdiction subject to applicable controls and provider safeguards.

## 14) Your Rights (Jurisdiction Dependent)

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to a supervisory authority. We may ask you to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

## 15) Children's Privacy

Alice is intended for users **18+** and is not directed to children.
