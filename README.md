# Alice — Terms & Privacy

**Effective Date:** 17/09/2026
**Last Updated:** 17/09/2026

This repo holds the rules of Alice. Before the legal wording, here is the plain-English guide.

## What are these documents?

- **[Terms.md](Terms.md) — the Terms of Service.** The contract between you and Alice. It says who may use the bot, what you may and may not do with it, and what happens if rules are broken. Using Alice means you agree to it.
- **[Privacy.md](Privacy.md) — the Privacy Policy.** It explains what data Alice handles (your messages, profile, memories), why she needs it, who else processes it, how long it is kept, and how to ask for deletion.

You accept both the first time you use Alice — via the **Agree and Continue** button, or the prompt shown in chat.

## The short version

- **18+ only.** Alice is not for children. Spicy content is only for users who accepted the Terms; everyone else gets refused.
- **Be lawful and decent.** No CSAM or anything involving minors (zero tolerance), no hate, harassment, threats, scams, malware, doxxing, or trying to jailbreak Alice's safeguards.
- **Alice has boundaries.** She can refuse, ignore you for ~10 minutes, or get you blacklisted. Apologizing sincerely can lift an ignore; repeat offenses won't be forgiven.
- **She remembers what you ask her to.** Up to 30 memories, removable any time — just tell her to forget. Chat history fades after ~3 hours.
- **Your messages are processed by AI.** Replies are generated by Gemma via Google AI Studio, which necessarily receives your messages and images. Top.gg only sees your ID to check votes. Nothing is sold; there are no ads.
- **Premium is manual.** No payments run through Alice, so there is nothing to refund or dispute.
- **Output can be wrong.** Don't treat it as professional advice — verify important things yourself.
